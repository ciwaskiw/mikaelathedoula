My Approach

Birth is not a medical event to be managed — it is a profound human experience to be witnessed with care.

Every family I support receives my undivided presence. I don't bring a script or a checklist — I bring years of training, deep listening, and a genuine belief that you already hold the strength you need. My role is simply to help you find it.

About Sarah

A familiar face for an unforgettable day.

I came to doula work after my own experience of feeling unsupported during labor — and realizing how much difference the right person in the room could have made. I became certified so I could be that person for others.

Outside of supporting families, I live in Baltimore with my husband and two kids. I believe deeply in community, in the power of women supporting women, and in the quiet magic of the early postpartum days.

I hold certification through DONA International and have supported over 60 families through birth and the postpartum period.
