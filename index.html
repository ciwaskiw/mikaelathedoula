About Mikaela

A familiar face for an unforgettable day.

I'm Mikaela Iwaskiw, a DONA-certified birth doula based in Gwynn Oak, Maryland. Over the past five years I've had the privilege of supporting over 20 families through labor, birth, and the early days of parenthood.

I believe every birthing person deserves continuous, compassionate support and a knowledgeable advocate in their corner. Whether you're planning a hospital birth, a birth center experience, or a home birth with a midwife, I'm here for you.

I hold the CD(DONA) credential through DONA International, completed my training in January 2023, and am certified in First Aid and CPR. I'm happy to discuss payment plans to make sure support is accessible to you.
