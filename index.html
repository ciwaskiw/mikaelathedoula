About Mikaela

Supporting families through empathy and encouragement

I'm Mikaela Iwaskiw, a DONA-certified birth doula based in Baltimore, Maryland. I believe every birthing person deserves continuous, compassionate support and a knowledgeable advocate in their corner.

Whether you're planning a hospital birth, a birth center experience, or a home birth with a midwife, I'm here for you.

I hold the CD(DONA) credential through DONA International, completed my training in January 2023, and am certified in First Aid and CPR. I'm happy to discuss payment plans to make sure support is accessible to you.
